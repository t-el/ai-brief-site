**AI Brief Daily** publishes one deep report every morning (around 07:00 Casablanca time) covering the most important AI news of the previous 24 hours.

Each report is drafted automatically from public RSS feeds of AI labs, tech press and research sites, then structured into a TL;DR, 4–6 explained stories and one deep dive. Every story links to its sources so you can check the claims yourself.
